⚠ Connection problem ✕

Connect to Traccar

Credentials stay in this browser tab. Nothing is stored or sent elsewhere.

Traccar CORS error? Add <entry key='web.origin'>*</entry> to conf\traccar.xml and restart the Traccar service.

GraphHopper sends no CORS headers by default — point this at the small graphhopper-proxy.js port (8988), not GraphHopper's own port (8989), or browser requests will be blocked.

Firebase URL must match the Realtime Database used by the Driver App (both write to and read from /trips/).
